A size reference for your pet scan

Print at 100% / Actual size. Turn off Fit to page and browser headers and footers. Use white A4 or US Letter paper.

Measure the outside black square: 10 cm wide × 10 cm tall.

  1. Check with a ruler. If either side is not 10 cm, correct the print scale and print again. Keep the white border clear; do not cut into it.
  2. Place it beside your pet. Keep the paper flat and vertical, at the same distance from the camera as the part of the body being measured. Keep all four corners visible and uncovered.
  3. Capture a standing side view and front view. Hold the camera at your pet’s body height. Include the whole pet and marker in each view. For video, pause briefly at each view.
  4. Keep the view clear. Remove costumes. Photograph without a gate or bars between the camera and pet. Do not attach the marker to your pet.

Reference ID 137 · Original ArUco 5×5 code with a black border. This marker supplies a known scale; it does not by itself guarantee accurate body measurements. A marker closer to or farther from the camera than the body can change the result.
